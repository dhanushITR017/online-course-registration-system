BCNF Normalization Proof
  • 1NF (Atomicity): Eliminated repeating groups. All attributes hold single atomic values with compound candidate key (student_id, course_id).
  • 2NF (No Partial FDs): Decomposed tables so student demographics depend purely on student_id and course details depend purely on course_id.
  • 3NF (No Transitive FDs): Separated evaluation and grading into participation_records, eliminating transitive dependencies.
  • BCNF (Determinant is Superkey): Every determinant in all 4 relations (students, courses, enrollments, participation_records) is a candidate key.
